- HNetBit FLOPs/sample (HGRN blocks, from configs + measured ratios, Ch 7 Tab. flops): 150M 443.3 G; 350M 634.0 G → ratio 1.43 ≈ measured time ratio 1.4345. Dense equivalents: 840.7 / 2559.8 G (×3.05 ≈ params ×3.03); chunking removes 47.3% (150M) and 75.2% (350M) of block FLOPs.

**Important correction — bytes per token is 3.16, NOT 4.5**: the corpus-average bytes/token measured by the pipeline is 3.16 (derivable from train steps: 25e9 / (193,240 × 32 × 1280) = 3.158). Consequences:
- Transformer context per sample ≈ 1280 × 3.16 ≈ 4043 bytes vs 4096 bytes for byte models → contexts are nearly symmetric (no transformer advantage).
- Transformer `bytes_per_step` ≈ 129,400 (not 184,320).
- Do NOT reintroduce the old "5760 bytes" or "4.5 bytes/token" claims anywhere.

## LaTeX conventions for this document

- **Math mode**: Always use `^\prime` not `'` (babel spanish issue)
- **Named functions in math**: `\operatorname{RMSNorm}` or `\text{RMSNorm}`
- **Code in text**: `\texttt{func_name}`
- **Tables**: `\begin{table}[H]` with `\caption` and `\label`
- **Acronyms**: First use italic with parentheses: \textit{Root Mean Square Layer Normalization} (RMSNorm)
- **Section depth**: `\section` → `\subsection` → `\subsubsection` (3 levels max)
- **Cross-references**: always `\label`/`\ref`, never hardcoded numbers
- **Cross-chapter refs**: use the chapter labels `cap:introduccion`, `cap:arquitectura`, `cap:implementacion`, `cap:metodo`, `cap:resultados`, `cap:discusion`
